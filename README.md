Remember Primitive Mobs? Adapted Mobs is the definitive (and officially approved) spiritual successor to it!

![the creepers](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/H5xbZtYP/images/e6406d9ff505396a6189ba589a687253b2671dfa.png)

From revamped PM mobs to brand-new ones (coming in the future), Adapted Mobs will give you unique, vanilla-style mobs
for your modded world!

Reaper:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Drops: Gunpowder, TNT (Rare), Red Mystery Egg (very Rare)

Spawns: Nighttime, everywhere (Overworld)

Hostility: Hostile

Special Info: They throw small blocks of TNT at you, but run away when you get too close. Their explosions don't deal
terrain damage by default, but this can be enabled in the config settings. When charged, their TNT does damage blocks.

</details>

Seeper:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Drops: Gunpowder, Yellow Mystery Egg (very Rare)

Spawns: Nighttime, everywhere (Overworld)

Hostility: Passive until provoked

Special Info: They search for a single nearby hostile mob to follow and buff with strength and speed. When below half
health and with no hostile to buff, they switch to the offensive, attempting to explode.

</details>

Leaper:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Drops: Gunpowder, Light Blue Creeper Burst Firework Star (Rare), Blue Mystery Egg (very Rare)

Spawns: Nighttime, everywhere (Overworld)

Hostility: Hostile

Special Info: When close, they wait a bit before launching themselves upwards, crashing down to the ground and exploding
in your face! They will explode like regular creepers if they don't have enough space to launch.

</details>

**Tolerable Creepers Integration**:

If you also have [Tolerable Creepers](https://modrinth.com/mod/tolerable-creepers) installed, Adapted Mobs' three
creepers gain matching Creeper Spores. When exploding they leave behind a puff of themed spores, which spawn baby
creepie variants.

You can also get spores as throwable items if AM creepers die to an explosion.

![the creepers](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/H5xbZtYP/images/6f345f12353693013dfb7d38f03c4699b16fecb7.png)

Reaper Spores / Leaper Spores / Seeper Spores:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Obtained: Thrown by a Reaper/Leaper/Seeper's spore cloud or when an AM creeper is killed by an explosion

Special Info: Throw one to summon its matching creepie. Place and light a Spore Barrel for the same effect,
or plant the spore in a flower pot for an adorable decoration

</details>

Reapie:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Spawns: Thrown Reaper Spores or grown from a Reaper's spore cloud

Hostility: Hostile, but can't move

Special Info: Won't target or follow anything, but detonates shortly after landing. Doesn't damage terrain

</details>

Leapie:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Spawns: Thrown Leaper Spores or grown from a Leaper's spore cloud

Hostility: Hostile

Special Info: Waits a moment, then launches straight up and comes back down, exploding wherever it lands.

</details>

Seepie:

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

Spawns: Thrown Seeper Spores or grown from a Seeper's spore cloud

Hostility: Friendly when thrown or released from a barrel, Hostile when hatched from an exploding Seeper

Special Info: The friendly kind follows you and buffs you with Speed or Strength before popping; the hostile kind
explodes and afflicts you with Slowness or Weakness

</details>
